0002 — three id namespaces

  • Status: active
  • Date: 2026-10-15 (retroactive)

Context

The sim must distinguish "the HC3's own devices" from "devices flua created for this run" so the hybrid dispatcher knows what to serve locally and what to forward. QAs, child devices, and the HC3's existing devices all share one /devices space.

Decision

Three disjoint ranges:

  • < 5000 — the HC3's namespace (seeded house devices, real devices online). 404s here forward to the HC3 when a remote backend exists.
  • ≥ 5000 — engine-assigned QA ids, starting at 5000, skipping taken ids. Always served locally (SimState.is_flua_id).
  • ≥ 900000 — runtime-created devices (FIRST_RUNTIME_ID), i.e. children from createChildDevice. Never collides with QA ids.

Consequences

  • Api._dispatch decides the 404-fallback purely from the id range — no per-entity lookup needed.
  • A proxy QA breaks the neat range on purpose: it runs under its HC3 id, so the sim shadow and the real device are one id (see the proxy invariants in invariants.md).
