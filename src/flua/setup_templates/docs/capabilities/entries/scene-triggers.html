Scenes — COND triggers and sourceTrigger, testable in the sim

--%%scene:true runs a QA as an HC3 Lua scene: a COND table of conditions (device properties, date/cron, custom events, operator any/all) and a body of actions.

  • On every matching refreshState event the scene runs its actions with sourceTrigger and _sceneId in scope (a manual execute triggers with {type="manual"}) — the HC3 semantics.
  • Date triggers evaluate os.date at the fire time, not boot time (pinned against the scheduled-evaluation bug).
  • flua simulates the trigger feed offline, so a scene is unit-testable like any QA: inject events, run on the virtual clock, assert actions.
  • The HC3 block editor is the wizard for scene-style automations; flua's role is convert → test → understand (ADR 0013).
